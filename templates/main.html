Our story

Built to close the financial literacy gap.

Hedgex was built in junior year of high school. The stock market was fascinating — but every learning resource was either too advanced, too vague, or locked behind a paywall. So the platform got built from scratch.

What started as a PCIS capstone project grew into a full paper trading platform with real market data, structured lessons, and tools that mirror actual brokerage apps. The mission is simple: make investing education accessible to everyone.

I created Hedgex because I wanted to give anyone the opportunity to take control of their financial future through the world of investing.

— Tanish Chauhan, Founder
