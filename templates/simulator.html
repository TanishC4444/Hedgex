MA Crossover (20/50)
Trend Following
Buy when the 20-day MA crosses above the 50-day. Sell on cross below. The most widely used systematic trend strategy.
Golden/Death Cross (50/200)
Trend Following
Buy on 50MA crossing above 200MA ("Golden Cross"). Sell on cross below ("Death Cross"). Slow, long-term signal watched by institutions.
RSI Reversal (30/70)
Mean Reversion
Buy when RSI-14 drops below 30 (oversold). Sell when RSI rises above 70 (overbought). Works best in sideways, range-bound markets.
RSI Trend Filter
Trend + Momentum
Only enter when RSI is 40–60 AND price is above the 50-day MA. Combines trend direction with momentum to filter false entries.
MACD Signal Cross
Trend + Momentum
Buy when MACD line (12/26 EMA) crosses above the signal line (9-day EMA). Sell on cross below. Classic momentum-trend combo indicator.
Momentum Breakout (20-day)
Momentum
Buy when price makes a new 20-day high. Exit when price falls 7% below the recent peak (trailing stop). Chases stocks making new highs.
Bollinger Band Reversion
Mean Reversion
Buy when price closes below the lower Bollinger Band (20-day MA minus 2 std dev). Exit when price returns to the 20-day MA center.
Volatility Breakout (ATR)
Volatility
Buy when price surges more than 1.5× the 14-day Average True Range in one session — a volatility expansion signal. Exits at 2× ATR stop loss.
Dollar Cost Averaging
Passive
Invest a fixed amount every 20 trading days regardless of price. Removes timing risk. Compare this to lump-sum Buy & Hold to see which wins.
Buy & Hold (Benchmark)
Benchmark
Buy the full position on day one and hold to period end. The gold standard benchmark — most active strategies fail to beat this long-term.
